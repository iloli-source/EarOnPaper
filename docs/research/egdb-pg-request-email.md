Subject: Request for EGDB-PG dataset access (research use)

Dear EGDB-PG team,

I am writing to request access to the EGDB-PG dataset (and EGDB-NDSP if available), as described in your paper "Towards Generalizability to Tone and Content Variations in the Transcription of Amplifier Rendered Electric Guitar Audio" (arXiv:2504.07406).

I am developing an open-source automatic guitar transcription engine (project "EarOnPaper" / engine "Pitchsieve"), which aims to make transcription accessible to non-musicians. Our current work focuses on improving note-level accuracy on amplifier-rendered (high-gain) electric guitar audio, evaluated against composer-verified ground-truth recordings. Your dataset's 256 amplifier/cabinet tone renderings would be used for research purposes only: fine-tuning and evaluating transcription models for tone generalization, following the training methodology described in your paper.

We will of course cite your paper in any resulting publication or documentation.

Thank you very much for making this resource available to the community.
