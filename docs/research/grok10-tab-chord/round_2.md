## Actionable quality criteria (usable TAB checklist)

Synthesized from what guitarists praise, mock, and patch by ear:

### 1. Pitch & fretting truth
- Correct notes/frets for the **recording you claim to tab**
- Correct **key** and capo
- Correct **tuning** stated up front (standard, drop, open, 7-string, etc.)
- Prefer **playable positions** (realistic fingerings / shifts), not random same-pitch frets

### 2. Harmony completeness (chords matter)
- Real chord quality: sus, add9, slash chords, inversions—not “everything is major”
- Chord symbols **above** the tab when the part is harmonic, not only frets
- Mark **rhythm vs lead** (or separate files)

### 3. Rhythm is non-negotiable
- Note values or clear **spacing/time grid** (ASCII distance, notation, or GP/Songsterr playback)
- Tempo/BPM + feel (swing, half-time, rests)
- Hard Times joke is the standard: frets without tempo/rhythm ≈ unusable

### 4. Structure & navigation
- Sections labeled: intro / verse / chorus / solo / bridge / outro
- Repeat marks / D.S. / simple form map
- Correct **part** (guitar 1 vs 2, riff vs fill)

### 5. Articulation & “sounds like the record”
- Palm mutes, slides, bends (amount), vibrato, hammer-ons/pull-offs, harmonics
- Pick-hand vs fret-hand muting clarity (**@LPGuitar_** treats muting as QoL)
- Dynamics where they define the riff

### 6. Multi-instrument / arrangement context
- Bass/drums (or at least chord + riff alignment) so timing isn’t guessed in a vacuum
- Playback synced to audio/YouTube when possible (Songsterr culture; UG catching up)

### 7. Presentation / readability
- Consistent string order, monospaced alignment, **sane spacing**
- Low visual clutter; readable on phone **without** trapping the tab behind ads/paywalls for a first look
- Chord diagrams only when they help (beginners), not instead of accurate frets

### 8. Provenance & verification
- **Official / author-approved / GP from trusted transcriber** > anonymous top-rated free tab
- Ratings alone are weak (popular wrong tabs stay popular)
- Ear-check against the recording; treat free tabs as a **draft**, not gospel

### 9. Setup constraints stated once
- Tuning, capo, string count, alternate parts, needed gear (e.g. “needs 7-string / low B”)
- Avoid silent wrong assumptions that waste practice time

## One-line “pro TAB” definition (from the discourse)

**A high-quality TAB is a playable, part-correct transcription of the actual recording: true pitch + real harmony + readable rhythm + articulations + setup headers—preferably multi-track or playback-synced—and ideally official or ear-verified, not merely popular.**

---

### Caveat
X discourse is heavy on **UG inaccuracy / Songsterr usability / “finish by ear”** and lighter on formal engraving rules. For engraving-level standards, pro books (e.g. Periphery official books, band-issued tabs) are the bar players point to when free web tabs fail.
